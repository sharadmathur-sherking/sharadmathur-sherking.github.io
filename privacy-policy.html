Privacy Policy for Aether

Effective Date: 11 June 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Aether ("the App"). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use Aether.

2. Information We Collect

a. Account Information

When you sign in using Google Sign-In or Email Authentication, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email Address
  • Google Profile Photo (if available)

b. User Content

Aether may store user-created data such as:

  • Notes and Study Folders
  • To-do Tasks and Timetables
  • Attendance and CGPA Records
  • Study Timer and Stopwatch Data
  • Productivity Statistics, Badges, and Streaks
  • AI Academic Doubts and Flashcard Decks

c. Device and Usage Information

To improve app performance, we may collect limited technical information such as device type, operating system, crash reports, and usage statistics.

d. Advertising Data

Aether uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect advertising identifiers and device information according to Google's advertising policies.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and maintain app features
  • Synchronize user data securely across devices
  • Authenticate users using Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Display advertisements through Google AdMob

4. Data Storage and Security

User data is securely stored using Firebase services provided by Google (Firestore, Authentication, and Cloud Storage). We take reasonable measures to protect user information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

5. SRM Attendance Sync Feature

Aether includes an optional feature that allows users to fetch attendance information from the SRM University portal.

  • Users voluntarily enter their SRM portal credentials.
  • Credentials are used only to access the user's attendance information.
  • Attendance data is fetched and displayed within the application.
  • Aether is not affiliated with or endorsed by SRM University.

6. Third-Party Services

Aether may interface with the following third-party services:

  • Google Sign-In
  • Firebase Authentication
  • Firebase Firestore
  • Firebase Storage
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Gemini AI (for academic doubt solving)

These services may collect and process information according to their own privacy policies.

7. Children's Privacy

Aether is intended for students and general users. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate parental consent where required.

8. User Rights

Users maintain full control over their personal data and may:

  • Access and export their stored information
  • Permanently delete their account and data at any time
  • Cease using the application

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page along with an updated effective date.

10. Account Deletion & Data Retention

Users have the right to request full, permanent deletion of their account and all associated cloud data at any time. We provide two methods:

  • In-App Deletion (Instant): Open Aether > Settings > Account > Delete Account & Data. This immediately purges all notes, attendance records, study logs, and credentials from our Firebase servers.
  • Web Deletion Portal (If Uninstalled): Visit our automated deletion page at https://sharadmathur-sherking.github.io/aether-delete-account/ to submit a one-click deletion request.

Upon deletion, all user content stored in Firebase Firestore and Cloud Storage is permanently erased.

11. Usage Access Information

If granted by the user, Aether may access app usage statistics and information about installed applications to provide screen time tracking, productivity insights, app categorization, and study analytics. This information is processed locally on the user's device unless otherwise stated.
